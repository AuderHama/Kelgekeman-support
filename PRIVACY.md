# Kelgekeman Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 5, 2026

This policy describes the current iOS release of **کێڵگەکەمان (Kelgekeman)**, published by Awder Hama.

## Information used by the game

Kelgekeman is an offline, single-player game. It does not require a game account and does not send gameplay, saved progress, or settings to the developer. Progress, preferences, and game state are saved locally on your device.

The current release does not include advertising, third-party analytics, in-app purchases, multiplayer, or a developer-operated cloud-save service. It does not use your contacts, location, microphone, or camera, and it does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

## Support messages

If you contact **auderhama0@gmail.com**, we receive the email address, message, and any attachments you choose to send. We use them to answer your request and investigate reported problems. These messages are handled by the email provider used for support. Please avoid sending sensitive personal information.

Support messages are kept only as needed to respond to the request and maintain necessary support records. You can contact us to request deletion of a support message you sent.

## Device storage and backups

You control the game's locally stored data through your device. Removing the app can remove its local saved progress. Any device-level backup or restoration is managed by your device and Apple settings; the developer does not receive those backups through the game.

Apple separately processes App Store transactions, downloads, and any information you choose to share with Apple under [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Changes

If a future release changes these practices, this policy will be updated with a new effective date.

## Contact
