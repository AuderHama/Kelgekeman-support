# کێڵگەکەمان — Kelgekeman Support

Kelgekeman is an offline, single-player farming and cave adventure in Sorani Kurdish.

For help, bug reports, or privacy questions, email **auderhama0@gmail.com**.

Please include your device model, iOS version, game version, and a short description of the problem. A screenshot can help, but please remove any personal information before sending it. Never send your Apple password or verification codes.

## Saved progress

Progress and settings are stored locally on your device. The game does not provide its own account system or cloud-save service. Removing the app can remove its local saved progress. Device backups and restoration are managed by your device and Apple settings.
